## Why This Milestone Matters

Linux has been a constant throughout my engineering career, from telecom production support to cloud platforms and factory infrastructure. This milestone grounds the rest of my certification journey in the systems those platforms run on.

## Learning Focus

- Managing services, users, permissions, storage, and networking.
- Investigating system issues through logs and command-line tools.
- Automating recurring administration and maintenance tasks.
