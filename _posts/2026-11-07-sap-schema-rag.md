## Overview

A completed LLMOps and retrieval-augmented generation platform focused on SAP database metadata. Its purpose is to help users find and understand table and column information through natural-language questions.

## The Problem

Working with an ERP database often starts with finding the right table, interpreting a column, and understanding the context needed for an integration or report. This platform brings that schema information into a retrieval-oriented workflow.

## Engineering Focus

- Discovering SAP table and column information.
- Retrieving relevant schema context for a question.
- Applying LLMOps and RAG concepts to enterprise metadata.
