## Academic Reference

This published Concordia ECE capstone connects wearable sensing with a monitoring backend to support first-responder safety. The university archive lists it as completed in 2022 by **Group 2022-13**, supervised by **R. Coutinho**.

## Technical Theme

The project combines physiological and location data, wireless communication, and backend visualization. Its listed tools include microcontrollers, Bluetooth and LoRa transceivers, and sensing devices.
