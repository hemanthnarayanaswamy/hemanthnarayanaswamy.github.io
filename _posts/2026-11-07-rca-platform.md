## Overview

An autonomous AI SRE and microservices platform centered on root-cause analysis. The project brings incident context and service-level investigation into a focused platform for production troubleshooting.

## Engineering Focus

- Root-cause analysis across a microservices environment.
- AI-assisted investigation and operational context.
- A platform approach to SRE workflows and production debugging.

## Project Context

This completed project reflects my interest in understanding how distributed systems fail and turning investigation into a more repeatable engineering workflow.
