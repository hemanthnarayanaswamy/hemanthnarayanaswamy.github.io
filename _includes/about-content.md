## My Story

My work brings together **platform engineering, site reliability, and automation** across Linux-based cloud and on-premises systems. I build on **AWS and Google Cloud**, using Kubernetes, Terraform, and CI/CD to make infrastructure easier to operate and scale. Python is a core part of my automation toolkit, alongside SQL, and my experience includes building ML training pipelines for production environments.

I enjoy the investigative side of engineering: following logs, connecting metrics, and digging into system behavior until I understand what's going wrong. From there, I look for ways to automate the fix, reduce downtime, and make the next incident easier to diagnose. I'm always learning and exploring better ways to design and run infrastructure.

Outside of work, I enjoy **anime** and taking on new **cloud and tooling certification challenges**.
